# Unit 2: Humans and the Environment

## Learning goals

Help a Grade 10 English learner discuss green living, environmental problems, plans, predictions, and practical advice.

## Core context

Nam plans to attend the first meeting of his school's Go Green Club. The Youth Union set up the club to improve the environment and encourage a greener lifestyle. Members are going to clean up the school after the opening ceremony. Nam predicts that the club will organise more activities to raise local awareness of environmental issues. Mike wants to reduce his carbon footprint and asks to join.

## Useful language

- carbon footprint, eco-friendly, litter, household appliances, energy
- green lifestyle, environmental issue, natural resources
- raise awareness, clean up the school, reduce waste
- set up a club, protect the environment, recycle used items

## Grammar focus

- Be going to for an existing plan: "We are going to clean up the school."
- Will for a prediction or decision made while speaking: "I think we will organise more activities."
- Passive voice when the action is more important than the actor: "The club was set up by the Youth Union."
- Advice: "You should..."; "If I were you, I would..."; "How about...?"

## Pronunciation focus

Practise the consonant blends /kl/, /pl/, /gr/, and /pr/. Useful examples include clean and club; please and plant; green and group; practise, present, and protect. Ask the learner to pronounce the complete beginning of each word clearly.

## Expanded vocabulary and reading ideas

Connect environmental vocabulary to concrete choices: switch off unused appliances, choose local or organic products, reduce single-use plastic, reuse bags and bottles, recycle materials, plant trees, add rubbish bins, and organise clean-up events. Discuss the result of each action rather than memorising a list.

## Skills progression

- Reading: distinguish green and non-green behaviour, identify a text's best title, and infer word meaning from context.
- Speaking: recommend actions with should/shouldn't and explain consequences with because, so, or This will.
- Listening: identify the responsibilities of clean-up, donation, and media teams at a green event.
- Writing: build a suggestion paragraph with a topic sentence, sequencers such as First and Second, an example, an expected result, and a short conclusion.
- Everyday English: ask for advice ("What should I do?", "What would you do?") and give advice politely.
- CLIL: explain a carbon footprint as greenhouse-gas emissions connected to a person's activities and propose realistic ways to reduce it.

## Additional practice prompts

1. Contrast a decision made now with an environmental plan made earlier using will and be going to.
2. Change an active environmental sentence into the passive voice.
3. Propose a three-team plan for a school green event.
4. Write a short paragraph suggesting two improvements for the school environment and their expected results.

## Practice and review guidance

Ask for specific, realistic actions at home or school. Encourage the learner to explain the expected result using "because", "so", or "This will...". Correct one key grammar or word-choice issue and provide a concise improved answer.

Reference answers from the source context: the Youth Union set up the club; its goals are environmental improvement and greener lifestyles; Nam expects more awareness-raising activities.
